# Research paper writing | 论文写作

Use the research problem to organize the argument, then choose English expressions by the job each sentence performs. Follow the user's assignment, discipline, target journal and existing manuscript structure when available. Explain edits in the user's language and deliver English prose when requested.

## Workflow

1. Establish the task and genre. Identify the stage (proposal, completed study, or revision), intended audience, section, research question, study design and material the author supplied. Work with what is available. Mark missing facts where they materially limit the deliverable.
2. Connect the question to the evidence. For an outline or structural rewrite, map `research question → data/source → analysis → finding → warranted interpretation → boundary`. For a local edit, trace the sentences that carry the argument using the same logic. Use the requested structure, including integrated Results and Discussion, or organize review and argumentative papers around problems or themes.
3. Shape paragraphs around one controlling idea. Give readers the evidence and the reasoning that connects it to the paragraph's claim. Choose a rhetorical move, a precise verb and the smallest necessary qualifier. Reuse the author's established terms and preserve comparators, direction, units, uncertainty and scope, including when translating from Chinese.
4. Verify the deliverable. Check that the abstract and closing answer the question posed in the introduction, that each main claim has support, and that caveats qualify the relevant inference. Keep proposals prospective. Preserve all verified facts and distinguish planned analyses, observations, interpretations and recommendations.

## Evidence and deliverables

- Return an evidence-linked outline for planning. Return the requested draft or revised section for writing. Add a short note for substantive changes or unresolved evidence gaps. When reporting a gap in a draft, identify the needed evidence or information in brackets instead of filling it with guesswork.
- Preserve the author's evidence, attributions and meaning. Verify any newly introduced paper and its support for the claim before citing it. Mark unverified citation needs in the draft. Keep claims of priority, causal mechanism, effectiveness, statistical significance and generalizability within their support.
- When presenting journal literature to this user, first identify the journal, the verified Journal Impact Factor with its year, and the Chinese Academy of Sciences journal partition with its edition and major/minor category as applicable. Label unavailable or unverified values and mark metrics for non-journal sources as not applicable. Evaluate support using the work's design and findings.
